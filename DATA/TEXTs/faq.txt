FUTURETECH UNIVERSITY
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
=================================
Last Updated: July 15, 2026
Maintained by: Office of Student Affairs, Building C, Room C-050

This document answers common questions from prospective students, current students,
faculty, and parents. For topics not covered here, consult the relevant policy
document (student_handbook.pdf, admission_guide.pdf, tuition_policy.pdf,
academic_regulations.pdf, library_rules.pdf) or contact the appropriate office
listed in campus_services.docx.

=================================
SECTION 1: ADMISSIONS
=================================

Q1: What are the minimum requirements to apply to FutureTech University?
A1: Applicants must hold a recognized secondary school certificate (or equivalent)
with a minimum overall grade of 75%, along with satisfactory results on the FTU
Admission Assessment. Full details are in admission_guide.pdf, Section 2
"Admission Requirements."

Q2: When is the application deadline for the Fall 2026 semester?
A2: The regular application deadline for Fall 2026 is August 15, 2026. A late
application window remains open until August 29, 2026 with a $50 late
application fee. See admission_guide.pdf, Section 5 "Application Deadlines."

Q3: Can international students apply to FutureTech University?
A3: Yes. International students follow the same general process but must also
submit a certified secondary school equivalency evaluation, a valid passport
copy, proof of English proficiency (IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 79 minimum), and
proof of financial support. See admission_guide.pdf, Section 4.

Q4: Is there an entrance exam?
A4: Applicants to Engineering, Computer Science, and Artificial Intelligence &
Data Science programs must sit the FTU Admission Assessment covering mathematics
and logical reasoning. Business Administration and Architecture applicants
submit a portfolio or personal statement instead. Details are in
admission_guide.pdf, Section 6 "Admission Process."

Q5: How do I check my application status?
A5: Log into the Applicant Portal at apply.futuretech.edu.eg using the reference
number emailed to you after submission. Status updates are also sent by email
from admissions@futuretech.edu.eg.

Q6: Can I transfer credits from another university?
A6: Yes, subject to review by the relevant department chair. A maximum of 60
credit hours may be transferred, and a minimum grade of C is required for any
transferred course. Contact the Registrar's Office for a transfer credit
evaluation.

Q7: Does FutureTech University offer conditional admission for students who
have not yet met the English proficiency requirement?
A7: Yes. Conditional admission is available with enrollment in the Academic
English Bridge Program for one semester prior to beginning degree coursework.

Q8: What documents are required at the time of application?
A8: Secondary school certificate, official transcript, passport-sized photos,
copy of national ID or passport, and (for international applicants) equivalency
certification. The complete checklist is in admission_guide.pdf, Section 3
"Required Documents."

=================================
SECTION 2: TUITION AND FINANCES
=================================

Q9: How much is tuition per semester?
A9: Tuition varies by college. As of the 2026-2027 academic year, tuition
ranges from $2,300 to $3,400 per semester depending on the program. Full
breakdown is in tuition_policy.pdf, Section 1 "Tuition Fees."

Q10: Can I pay tuition in installments?
A10: Yes. FutureTech University offers a 3-installment plan (40% / 30% / 30%)
with a $25 administrative fee. See tuition_policy.pdf, Section 3 "Installment
Plans."

Q11: What happens if I pay tuition late?
A11: A late payment penalty of 2% of the outstanding balance is applied for
each week the payment is overdue, up to a maximum of 10%. Students with unpaid
balances after 30 days may face registration holds. See tuition_policy.pdf,
Section 5 "Late Payment Penalties."

Q12: What is the refund policy if I withdraw from a course?
A12: Refunds are calculated on a sliding scale: 100% refund if withdrawn
within the first week of the semester, 75% within the second week, 50% within
the third week, and no refund after the fourth week. See tuition_policy.pdf,
Section 4 "Refund Policy," and note that withdrawal from courses is also
governed by academic_regulations.pdf, Section 4 "Withdrawal Policy."

Q13: Where is the Financial Office located?
A13: The Financial Office is located in Building C, Room C-020, open Sunday
through Thursday, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Contact: finance@futuretech.edu.eg,
+20 2 2589 3200.

Q14: Are there scholarships available?
A14: Yes, FutureTech University offers Merit-Based, Need-Based, Athletic, and
Departmental Scholarships. See scholarship_info.txt for eligibility and
required GPA for each type.

Q15: Does financial aid cover housing and books?
A15: Standard scholarships cover tuition only. The Full-Ride Presidential
Scholarship additionally covers on-campus housing; textbooks are not covered
under any scholarship category.

SECTION 3: ACADEMIC POLICIES
=================================

Q16: What GPA do I need to remain in good academic standing?
A16: Students must maintain a cumulative GPA of 2.0 or higher. Falling below
2.0 results in Academic Probation. See academic_regulations.pdf, Section 2
"Academic Probation."

Q17: How is the GPA calculated?
A17: FutureTech University uses a 4.0 scale. Each letter grade corresponds to
a grade point value (A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, and so on) multiplied by
credit hours, summed, and divided by total credit hours attempted. Full grading
scale is in academic_regulations.pdf, Section 1.

Q18: What happens if I miss a final exam?
A18: Students who miss a final exam due to a documented medical or personal
emergency may request a make-up exam through the Registrar's Office within 5
working days of the missed exam. See academic_regulations.pdf, Section 6
"Make-up Exams."

Q19: How many absences are allowed per course?
A19: Students may not exceed 20% unexcused absences in any course. Exceeding
this limit results in a "WF" (Withdrawn-Failing) grade. See
student_handbook.pdf, Section 4 "Attendance Policy."

Q20: Can I appeal a final grade?
A20: Yes. Grade appeals must be submitted in writing to the Office of the
Registrar within 10 working days of grade posting. See academic_regulations.pdf,
Section 8 "Appeals Process."

Q21: What are the graduation requirements?
A21: Students must complete all required credit hours for their major
(typically 132-152 credit hours depending on the program), maintain a
cumulative GPA of at least 2.0, and complete a graduation project or
internship. See student_handbook.pdf, Section 6 "Graduation Requirements."

Q22: How do I withdraw from a course without academic penalty?
A22: Course withdrawal must be submitted before the end of Week 10 of the
semester and results in a "W" grade, which does not affect GPA. Withdrawals
after Week 10 result in a "WF." See academic_regulations.pdf, Section 4.

Q23: What is Academic Probation and how long does it last?
A23: Academic Probation is a formal warning status for students whose
cumulative GPA falls below 2.0. Students remain on probation for one semester,
during which they must raise their GPA to 2.0 or above or face suspension.

SECTION 4: STUDENT LIFE AND CONDUCT
=================================

Q24: What are my rights as a student at FutureTech University?
A24: Student rights, including the right to fair evaluation, freedom of
expression within university guidelines, and access to grievance procedures,
are detailed in student_handbook.pdf, Section 2 "Student Rights."

Q25: What behavior violates the Student Code of Conduct?
A25: Violations include academic dishonesty (cheating, plagiarism), harassment,
property damage, and unauthorized access to university systems. See
student_handbook.pdf, Section 5 "Student Code of Conduct."

Q26: Where can I report an incident of harassment or misconduct?
A26: Reports can be filed confidentially with the Office of Student Affairs
(Building C, Room C-050) or through the online reporting form on the student
portal.

Q27: Is there a dress code at FutureTech University?
A27: There is no strict dress code, but attire must be appropriate for an
academic and professional environment; safety attire is mandatory in
engineering labs and workshops.

Q28: What clubs and student organizations are available?
A28: FutureTech University hosts over 25 student organizations including the
Robotics Club, AI Society, Entrepreneurship Club, Debate Society, and multiple
sports teams. Registration occurs through the Student Affairs Office each
September.

SECTION 5: LIBRARY SERVICES
=================================

Q29: What are the library's opening hours?
A29: The Main Library (Library Building, Ground to 3rd Floor) is open Sunday
through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Closed on Fridays. See library_rules.pdf, Section 3 "Opening Hours."

Q30: How many books can I borrow at a time?
A30: Undergraduate students may borrow up to 5 books for 14 days; graduate
students may borrow up to 8 books for 21 days. See library_rules.pdf, Section
2 "Borrowing Rules."

Q31: What is the penalty for a late book return?
A31: A fine of $0.50 per day per book is charged, up to a maximum of $15 per
item. See library_rules.pdf, Section 5 "Late Return Penalties."

Q32: Can I reserve a study room?
A32: Yes. Study rooms may be reserved for up to 3 hours per day via the
library portal or the front desk on the Ground Floor. See library_rules.pdf,
Section 6 "Study Room Reservation."

Q33: Does the library provide access to digital journals and databases?
A33: Yes. The library subscribes to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink,
and JSTOR, accessible on campus or remotely via the library VPN with your
student credentials. See library_rules.pdf, Section 4 "Digital Resources."

SECTION 6: CAMPUS SERVICES
=================================

Q34: How do I get IT support for my student account or Wi-Fi issues?
A34: Contact the IT Support Desk in Building B, Room B-010, or email
itsupport@futuretech.edu.eg. Walk-in hours are Sunday through Thursday,
8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. See campus_services.docx, Section 1.

Q35: Where is the on-campus medical clinic and what services does it offer?
A35: The Medical Clinic is located on the Ground Floor of the Student Center,
open Sunday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, offering first aid, general
consultations, and referrals to partner hospitals. See campus_services.docx,
Section 3.

Q36: Does the university provide transportation?
A36: Yes. FutureTech University operates shuttle buses on 6 routes covering
major districts, departing every 30 minutes from 6:30 AM to 8:30 PM. Route
maps and schedules are available from the Transportation Office in the
Student Center. See campus_services.docx, Section 4.

Q37: What dining options are available on campus?
A37: The main cafeteria in the Student Center serves breakfast, lunch, and
dinner, and there are additional cafés in Building A and Building C. See
campus_services.docx, Section 5.

Q38: How do I apply for on-campus housing?
A38: Applications for on-campus housing are submitted through the Housing
Office (Student Center, Room SC-102) each semester; priority is given to
first-year and international students. See campus_services.docx, Section 6.

Q39: Does the university offer career counseling and internship placement?
A39: Yes, the Career Center (Building C, Room C-060) offers resume workshops,
mock interviews, and an internship placement program with over 150 partner
companies. See campus_services.docx, Section 7.

SECTION 7: MISCELLANEOUS / AMBIGUOUS QUERIES
=================================
The following are examples of common but ambiguous questions students ask;
answering them well typically requires clarifying which office, policy, or
document the student actually means.

Q40: "How do I get my money back?"
A40: This depends on context. If referring to a course withdrawal refund, see
tuition_policy.pdf, Section 4 "Refund Policy." If referring to a scholarship
overpayment or duplicate payment, contact the Financial Office directly at
finance@futuretech.edu.eg.

Q41: "Where do I go if I have a problem?"
A41: This depends on the type of problem. Academic issues (grades, exams) go
to the Registrar's Office; conduct or harassment issues go to Student Affairs;
technical issues go to IT Support; billing issues go to the Financial Office.
See campus_services.docx for a full office directory.

Q42: "Can I still register?"
A42: This depends on the deadline in question. Course registration for Fall
2026 closes on September 5, 2026 (add/drop period). Late registration with a
$40 fee is allowed until September 12, 2026. After that date, registration
requires Dean's approval.

Q43: "I missed something, what do I do?"
A43: If you missed a final exam, see academic_regulations.pdf, Section 6
"Make-up Exams." If you missed a payment deadline, see tuition_policy.pdf,
Section 5 "Late Payment Penalties." If you missed an admission deadline, see
admission_guide.pdf, Section 5.

Q44: What is the difference between Academic Probation and Suspension?
A44: Academic Probation is a warning status for one semester when GPA falls
below 2.0. Academic Suspension occurs if a student fails to restore their GPA
to 2.0 or above by the end of the probation semester, resulting in mandatory
withdrawal for at least one semester. See academic_regulations.pdf, Section 2.

Q45: How do I contact my academic advisor?
A45: Academic advisors are assigned by department. Contact information for
department chairs and faculty is listed in faculty_contacts.docx and
faculty_directory.csv. Students can also find their assigned advisor's name
on the student portal under "My Advisor."
