FUTURETECH UNIVERSITY
OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENTS
=======================
This feed is maintained by the Office of Communications and University
Relations. Announcements are archived for one academic year. For policy
details referenced below, consult the relevant document (student_handbook.pdf,
tuition_policy.pdf, academic_regulations.pdf, admission_guide.pdf,
library_rules.pdf).

-----------------------------------------
July 22, 2026
SUBJECT: Fall 2026 Course Registration Now Open
Course registration for the Fall 2026 semester is now open on the student
portal. The add/drop period closes on September 5, 2026. Late registration
will be permitted until September 12, 2026 with a $40 late registration fee.
Students on Academic Probation must obtain advisor approval before
registering. See academic_regulations.pdf, Section 2 for probation rules.

-----------------------------------------
July 18, 2026
SUBJECT: New Digital Library Resource - IEEE Xplore Expanded Access
The Main Library has expanded its subscription to IEEE Xplore to include
full conference proceedings dating back to 1990. Access is available on
campus or remotely through the library VPN. See library_rules.pdf, Section 4
"Digital Resources" for login instructions.

-----------------------------------------
July 15, 2026
SUBJECT: Scholarship Renewal Reminder
All current scholarship holders are reminded that the Renewal Request Form
is due by July 1 each year per scholarship_info.txt, Section 4 "Renewal
Rules." Students who have not yet submitted Spring 2026 transcripts for
review should do so immediately through the student portal to avoid a lapse
in scholarship disbursement for Fall 2026.

-----------------------------------------
July 10, 2026
SUBJECT: Building D Design Studio Renovation Complete
The renovation of the Department of Architecture & Design studio spaces in
Building D is now complete. New 3D printing and laser-cutting equipment is
available for student use starting Fall 2026. Orientation sessions will be
held during the first week of the semester; sign up through the department
office (Building D, Room D-101).

-----------------------------------------
July 5, 2026
SUBJECT: Tuition Fee Schedule Published for 2026-2027
The Financial Office has published the updated tuition fee schedule for the
2026-2027 academic year. Tuition ranges from $2,300 to $3,400 per semester
depending on program. See tuition_policy.pdf, Section 1 "Tuition Fees" for
the full breakdown by department. The 3-installment payment plan remains
available with a $25 administrative fee.

-----------------------------------------
June 30, 2026
SUBJECT: Presidential Full-Ride Scholarship Interviews Scheduled
Interviews for Presidential Full-Ride Scholarship applicants for the Fall
2026 intake will be held between July 6 and July 10, 2026, either in person
at Building C, Room C-025, or virtually upon request. Applicants will be
notified of interview times by email no later than July 2, 2026.

June 20, 2026
SUBJECT: Career Center Summer Internship Fair Recap
The Career Center's Summer Internship Fair hosted 45 partner companies and
connected over 600 students with internship opportunities. Students who
missed the fair can still browse open internship postings through the Career
Center portal. See campus_services.docx, Section 7 "Career Center."

-----------------------------------------
June 12, 2026
SUBJECT: Spring 2026 Final Exam Results Posted
Final grades for the Spring 2026 semester have been posted to the student
portal. Students wishing to appeal a grade must submit a written appeal to
the Office of the Registrar within 10 working days of this posting, per
academic_regulations.pdf, Section 8 "Appeals Process."
