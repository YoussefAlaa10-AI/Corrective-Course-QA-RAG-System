1. SCHOLARSHIP TYPES
==========================================

1.1 Presidential Full-Ride Scholarship
Covers 100% of tuition plus on-campus housing for the full duration of the
degree program (contingent on annual renewal). Awarded to no more than 10
incoming students per academic year, based on outstanding academic performance
and a competitive interview with the Scholarship Committee.

1.2 Merit-Based Scholarship
Covers 25%, 50%, or 75% of tuition depending on the applicant's secondary
school grade average and FTU Admission Assessment score. Renewable each
semester based on continued academic performance.

1.3 Need-Based Scholarship
Covers up to 50% of tuition for students who demonstrate financial hardship,
verified through submitted income documentation and a means assessment
conducted by the Office of Financial Aid.

1.4 Departmental Scholarship
Offered by individual departments (e.g., Department of Artificial Intelligence
& Data Science, Department of Engineering programs) to students who
distinguish themselves in department-specific competitions, research
assistantships, or teaching assistantships. Coverage varies from 10% to 40% of
tuition and is determined by the relevant department chair.

1.5 Athletic Scholarship
Covers 20% to 60% of tuition for students who represent FutureTech University
in inter-university sports competitions, subject to approval by the Athletics
Office and maintenance of minimum academic standing.

1.6 Sibling Discount (not a scholarship, but a related tuition benefit)
Families with two or more children concurrently enrolled at FutureTech
University receive a 10% tuition reduction on the second and subsequent
sibling's tuition. This benefit is administered by the Financial Office (see
tuition_policy.pdf, Section 1) rather than the Scholarship Committee.

2. ELIGIBILITY AND REQUIRED GPA
==========================================

Scholarship Type          | Minimum Entry GPA | Minimum Renewal GPA
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Presidential Full-Ride    | 3.9 / 4.0          | 3.7 / 4.0
Merit-Based (75% tier)    | 3.7 / 4.0          | 3.5 / 4.0
Merit-Based (50% tier)    | 3.4 / 4.0          | 3.2 / 4.0
Merit-Based (25% tier)    | 3.0 / 4.0          | 2.8 / 4.0
Need-Based                | 2.5 / 4.0          | 2.5 / 4.0
Departmental              | 3.2 / 4.0          | 3.0 / 4.0
Athletic                  | 2.3 / 4.0          | 2.3 / 4.0
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Note: These GPA figures apply to FutureTech University's 4.0 scale as defined
in academic_regulations.pdf, Section 1 "GPA System." Incoming freshman
applicants are evaluated using their secondary school grade average converted
to the equivalent scale by the Admissions Office rather than a university GPA,
since they have not yet completed university coursework.

All scholarship recipients must also remain in good academic standing as
defined in academic_regulations.pdf, Section 2 "Academic Probation." A student
placed on Academic Probation automatically loses scholarship eligibility for
the following semester, regardless of scholarship type.

==========================================
3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
==========================================

For all scholarship applications:
- Completed Scholarship Application Form (available on the student portal)
- Official transcript or secondary school certificate
- Copy of national ID or passport
- Personal statement (500 words maximum)

Additional documents by type:
- Need-Based Scholarship: Family income statement, utility bill, or
  equivalent proof of financial circumstances, and a signed Financial
  Hardship Declaration.
- Departmental Scholarship: Letter of recommendation from a faculty member
  in the relevant department, and evidence of the qualifying achievement
  (competition results, research output, etc.).
- Athletic Scholarship: Certification from a recognized sports federation or
  club, and a letter from the FutureTech University Athletics Office.
- Presidential Full-Ride Scholarship: All of the above general documents,
  plus two academic reference letters and attendance at an in-person or
  virtual interview.

==========================================
4. RENEWAL RULES
==========================================

- Scholarships are reviewed at the end of each academic year (after Spring
  semester grades are posted).
- Students must submit a Renewal Request Form through the student portal by
  July 1 each year.
- Failure to meet the minimum renewal GPA results in either a reduction to a
  lower scholarship tier (for Merit-Based awards) or full loss of the
  scholarship (for Presidential, Departmental, and Athletic awards).
- Students who withdraw from more than 2 courses in a single semester
  (see academic_regulations.pdf, Section 4 "Withdrawal Policy") may have
  their scholarship placed under review regardless of GPA.
- Need-Based Scholarships require households to resubmit income
  documentation annually, as financial circumstances are reassessed each
  year.
- A scholarship recipient found responsible for a violation of the Student
  Code of Conduct (student_handbook.pdf, Section 5) will have their
  scholarship revoked immediately, independent of academic performance.

==========================================
6. HOW SCHOLARSHIPS ARE DISBURSED
==========================================

Approved scholarships are applied directly as a credit against the tuition
invoice generated by the Financial Office before the start of each semester
(see tuition_policy.pdf, Section 2 "Payment Methods"). Scholarships never
result in a direct cash payment to the student. Any scholarship amount
exceeding the tuition due for a given semester is carried forward as a credit
toward the following semester and is not refunded in cash.

==========================================
7. FREQUENTLY CONFUSED SITUATIONS
==========================================

- A "full scholarship" refers only to the Presidential Full-Ride Scholarship;
  Merit-Based awards top out at 75% of tuition and are sometimes mistakenly
  referred to as "full" by students. Always confirm which award a student
  actually holds before advising them on remaining balance.
- Scholarship coverage applies to tuition fees only, not to the housing fees
  described in campus_services.docx, Section 6 "Housing Office," except in
  the case of the Presidential Full-Ride Scholarship, which explicitly
  includes on-campus housing.
